# What This Is

This is an app called Shelf.

Shelf lets you put your books on a computer so you can read them.

You can read your books on your computer. You can also read the same books on your phone.

When you stop reading, the app remembers the exact page you were on. The next time you open the book, it opens on that same page. You do not need to find your place again.

## What You Can Do

- **Add a book.** You put in a PDF file or an EPUB file.
- **Read the book.** The pages show on the screen. You scroll down to read more.
- **Make the screen full.** You can make the book fill the whole screen. This is called fullscreen.
- **Mark words in color.** You can pick words in the book and give them a color. This is called a highlight. This helps you remember important parts.
- **Draw on the page.** You can draw lines on the page with your mouse or your finger. You can circle things or write notes by drawing.
- **Erase marks.** If you want to remove a highlight or a drawing, you press the eraser tool. Then you touch the mark, and it goes away.
- **Ask a question.** If you read words you do not understand, you can pick those words. Then you press the Ask AI button. A computer helper will explain the words to you in a simple way.

## Why This App Is Useful

Before this app, you might read a book on your computer. Then your highlights and notes stay only on that one computer. If you go to your phone, your marks are not there.

With Shelf, your marks are saved on the internet, not just on one device. So when you open the same book on a different device, your highlights, your drawings, and your page are all still there.

## How It Is Built

- The part you see and touch is made with simple web code: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- The part that remembers your books and your marks is a database called MongoDB.
- The part that shows your PDF pages is a tool called PDF.js.
- The part that answers your questions is a computer helper that talks through the internet.
